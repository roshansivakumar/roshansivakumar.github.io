# The Future of Human-AI Collaboration

## Introduction

Exploring how artificial intelligence will reshape the way we work and think in the coming decades.

## Where We Are Now

AI is already embedded in our daily workflows — from code completion to medical imaging. But we're still in the early chapters of this story.

## What's Changing

- **From tool to partner**: AI is moving from passive tool to active collaborator
- **Augmentation over replacement**: The best outcomes come from human-AI teams
- **New skill requirements**: Understanding how to work *with* AI becomes a core competency

## What I Think About

The most interesting question isn't "what can AI do?" — it's "what should humans focus on when AI handles the rest?"
