# Teaching Kids to Think Like Engineers

## Introduction

Simple everyday activities that help develop an engineering mindset in children.

## Why Engineering Thinking Matters

Engineering thinking isn't just about building things — it's a way of approaching the world with curiosity, logic, and creativity.

## Activities to Try

- **Build challenges**: Give kids materials and a problem to solve
- **Take things apart**: Old electronics, clocks, and toys become learning tools
- **Ask "why" and "how"**: Encourage questioning everything around them

## Lessons Learned

The best part of teaching kids to think like engineers is rediscovering the fundamentals yourself.
