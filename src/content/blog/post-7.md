# Mom's One-Pot Military Mac & Cheese

## The Story

A hearty, cheesy comfort meal that can feed a platoon and still taste like home.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb elbow macaroni
- 4 cups shredded cheddar cheese
- 2 cups milk
- 4 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp flour
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions

1. Cook macaroni according to package directions, drain
2. In the same pot, melt butter and stir in flour to make a roux
3. Slowly add milk, stirring constantly until thickened
4. Add mustard powder, salt, and pepper
5. Stir in cheese until melted and smooth
6. Add macaroni back and stir to combine
7. Serve hot — no one can resist seconds

## Tips

- Add breadcrumbs on top and broil for 5 minutes for a crispy finish
- Throw in some diced jalapeños for a kick
